# PRODUCT.md

Strategic design context for this project. Read by the impeccable design skill
(`.github/skills/impeccable`) before any design work.

## Register

brand

This is a personal portfolio. The design is part of the product: the site
itself is evidence of the kind of work Andrew ships.

## Platform

web

## Users

- Recruiters and hiring managers scanning for real experience in data science,
  analytics, and engineering leadership.
- Peers and colleagues in data science, analytics, and finance who want to see
  concrete examples of work.
- People learning statistics and machine learning who land on the interactive
  demos.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio and demo site for Andrew Frankenreider, a data science
and analytics manager at Caterpillar, Inc. in the financial services division.
His team builds ML models, analytics dashboards, web applications, and process
automation (RPA) for accounting and finance and for sales and operations
planning (S&OP).

The site records his experience and hosts interactive, browser-based demos of
core methods (linear regression, k-means clustering, neural networks, market
data analysis).

## Positioning

A working practitioner's site, not a marketing page. Everything on it should
read like it was written by the person it describes.

## Voice

- First person, plain, specific. Say what the team builds and for whom.
- Concrete nouns over abstractions: "Power BI reporting", "Django APIs",
  "RPA bots", not "innovative solutions" or "data-driven insights".
- No taglines, no manifesto lines, no aspirational filler.

## Anti-references

- AI-generated portfolio copy: "built to explain, not impress", "crafted with
  passion", "turning data into insights", "seamless", "leverage", em-dash
  aphorisms, and paired-contrast slogans ("X, not Y").
- Generic SaaS landing pages: gradient blobs, centered hero + two buttons,
  identical stacked feature cards.
- Buzzword resumes.

## Design Principles

1. Content first: experience, demos, and contact details carry the site.
2. Quiet, precise surfaces; the interactive demos are where visual energy goes.
3. Every sentence must survive the question "would Andrew actually say this?"

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA contrast for body text.
- Keyboard focus visible everywhere; respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Minimum 44px touch targets on mobile.
